# Support tiers and response targets

**Support policy — effective 2026-07-01**

## First response targets by tier

| Tier | Channels | First response target | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | Email | **24 business hours** | Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 ET |
| Growth | Email, chat | **8 business hours** | Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 ET |
| Scale | Email, chat, phone escalation | **4 hours** | 24x5 |
| Enterprise | Email, chat, phone, named TAM | **1 hour** (Sev-1: **15 minutes**) | 24x7 |

These are *first response* targets, not resolution times.

## Tier-gated support actions

Some actions are only available through support on the higher tiers. The main one is the
**one-time 3-calendar-day dispute evidence extension**, which Scale and Enterprise accounts
may request before the original 5-day deadline expires. Starter and Growth accounts cannot be
granted an extension, and no tier can be granted one retroactively.

Enterprise accounts also get quarterly business reviews and a completed security questionnaire
on request.

## Incident communication

Meridian publishes live status and incident updates on the status page. For a confirmed
security or data incident affecting a merchant's data, Meridian notifies affected merchants
within **72 hours** of confirmation. A written root cause analysis follows within **10
business days** for any Sev-1.

## Scope of this document

This document covers **support responsiveness only**. It is not a service-availability
commitment: uptime obligations, availability percentages, and any service credits are set out
in the master services agreement and its order form, which are negotiated per account and are
not published here. Do not quote support response targets as an uptime guarantee.

## Escalation

Any tier may escalate a live payments outage by replying `SEV1` to an open ticket, which pages
the on-call engineer regardless of plan.
