# Core pricing — effective 2026-07-01

**Pricing sheet — US and EU accounts, online card acceptance**

| Tier | Monthly | Per successful card charge |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | USD 0 | **2.9% + USD 0.30** |
| Growth | USD 99 | **2.6% + USD 0.25** |
| Scale | USD 499 | **2.3% + USD 0.20** |
| Enterprise | Custom | Interchange++ from 0.35% + USD 0.15 |

Rates apply per *successful* charge. Declines and authorisations that are never captured are
free. Tier changes take effect on the next billing cycle.

## Other transaction pricing

- **Bank debit (ACH debit):** 0.8%, capped at USD 5.00 per charge, all tiers.
- **Failed ACH debit (returned charge):** USD 2.50 per return.
- **Dispute fee:** USD 15.00 per dispute, refunded in full if the merchant wins.
- **Instant Payouts:** 1.25%, minimum USD 0.50. Standard payouts are free on every tier.
- **Currency conversion:** see the FX and cross-border pricing sheet.
- **Marketplace/platform fees:** see the Connect pricing sheet.

## How refunds affect fees

When a charge is refunded, the **fixed per-transaction fee is never returned**. The
**percentage fee is returned only if the refund is issued within 48 hours of the charge**.
After 48 hours the merchant keeps the refund obligation and Meridian keeps the percentage
fee. This applies to partial refunds pro rata.

## What is included at every tier

Hosted Checkout, Vault tokenization, the Core API, webhooks, standard payouts, and the
dashboard. There is no setup fee, no PCI compliance fee, and no monthly minimum on Starter.
Support response targets differ by tier — see the support tiers doc.
